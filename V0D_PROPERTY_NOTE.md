Cadastral continuity caveat for future multi-block reconstruction:
A Swedish property can consist of more than one separate area/skifte. Therefore same fastighetsbeteckning must not be treated as a hard topological-continuity constraint. Roads, waterways, other properties or other land can separate areas belonging to the same property. Future reconstruction should prefer connected/nearby components but not require a single contiguous polygon. Exact Fastighetsindelning geometry, if available under the applicable Lantmäteriet access terms, is preferable to heuristic proximity reconstruction.
